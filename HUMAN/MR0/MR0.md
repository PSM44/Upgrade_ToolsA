# MR0 — Integrated Management Review
MR0 is a management control process, not an execution engine or authorization substitute. Review cross-domain portfolio (HARNESS, MEMORY, MODELS, AGENTS, TOOLING), roadmaps, risk, budget, execution truth, dependencies, candidate promotions, retirement and incidents.
## Rhythm
Weekly concise DELTA review; monthly deep portfolio and cost review; exceptional review for critical CVE, compromised provenance, unexpected paid charges or irrecoverable drift.
## Required inputs
`CONTINUITY/STATE.md`, Registry inventories, Radar changes, Arena evidence, vulnerability/licensing changes, cost and exposure reports, cross-project demand.
## Output contract
Date, observed baseline and evidence, accomplishments, blockers, risk register, decisions, responsible party, due date, independent execution fronts, safety gates, next review. Decisions recorded in `HUMAN/MR0/DECISION_LOG.md`; statuses NOT derived from self-report alone.
## KPI dashboard
TIME_TO_VALIDATED_CAPABILITY; PRODUCTION_VERIFICATION_RATE; TECHNOLOGY_FRESHNESS; HUMAN_INTERVENTIONS_PER_ADOPTION; COST_VARIANCE; ROLLBACK_SUCCESS.
