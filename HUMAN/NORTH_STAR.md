# Upgrade_TA — North Star and Mission
**AI Technology Intelligence & Lifecycle Management Platform**. Continuously discover, compare, experimentally verify, safely adopt, maintain and retire AI technologies used in individual projects or transversally.
## Mandate
1. Observe AI market, releases, pricing, license shifts, vulnerabilities and deprecations.
2. Maintain a versioned **Technology Registry**, a decision-oriented **Technology Radar**, and a reproducible **Technology Arena**.
3. Publish validated reusable capabilities (adapters, Skills, runbooks, integrations) with provenance and rollback.
4. Maintain deployed technology lifecycles (compatibility, updates, drift, security, costs, consumer projects, retirement).
5. Provide MR0 Integrated Management Review, HO.1 operational continuity, and a human-language Dictionary.
## Boundaries
Upgrade_TA controls technology qualifications and release recommendations. It **does not** supersede authority HUMAN > MORCH > RORCH > EXE in PS.NightShift; it does not automatically mutate other projects. New tools are not proven merely by install, import, mocked call or UI presence.
## Method
GOALS → LOOPS → GRAPH/DAG → AGENTS/SUBAGENTS_WHEN_USEFUL → CROSS_CHECK → TERMINAL_CONVERGENCE. MAX_SAFE_PARALLELISM; NO_TUNNEL_VISION. Independent fronts run in parallel; serialize only genuine dependencies and safety gates. Evidence-first, reversible, delta-only communication. User authorization for irreversible cleanup, external financial commitments, commit/push and changes to other repositories.
## Outcome metrics
Time to validated capability; real end-to-end acceptance rate; technology freshness; human intervention count; update rollback success; annualized cost; cross-project reuse; proportion with verifiable provenance.
## Industry influences
Thoughtworks Technology Radar rings; CNCF platform engineering/product thinking; DevSecOps, OpenSSF Scorecard and SLSA provenance; SPDX/CycloneDX SBOM; NIST SSDF and documented exceptions. Adopt concepts, do not claim external certification.
